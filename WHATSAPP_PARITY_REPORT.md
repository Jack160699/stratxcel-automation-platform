## Overall assessment

Real, working infrastructure exists end to end: webhook receipt → signature verification → phone-to-tenant routing → queue → async processing → lead creation → service classification → shadow response, all auditable via `/admin/platform/whatsapp`. **What does not exist** is the actual conversational depth of the legacy bot — booking, proposals, payment links, and escalation are either unwired or unbuilt. Cutting over today, even with the phone binding active, would be a functional regression for any conversation beyond first contact or opt-out.

**Recommendation:** keep shadow mode on (already the default — `shadow_mode: true` on every phone binding, `WHATSAPP_INTEGRATION_MODE` defaulting to `disabled`) until the four "not ported" rows above are addressed, or until you decide those flows aren't worth porting and the scope is deliberately narrowed.
