## 2. MRP Derivation Formula

$$\text{MRP} = \text{Base Compute Cost} + \text{Infrastructure Overhead (15\%)} + \text{Risk Reserve (10\%)} + \text{Target Gross Margin (60--70\%)} \times \text{Market Tier Factor}$$

### Market Tier Adjustments:
- **Tier 1 Cities** (Metro/Major hubs): 1.0x baseline.
- **Tier 2 Cities**: 0.95x competitive adjustment.
- **Tier 3 Cities**: 0.90x regional adjustment.
