# StratXcel Requirement Intelligence Engine

## 1. Role in the Architecture

The Requirement Intelligence Agent is the **core business intelligence engine** of StratXcel. It transforms raw observations and audit findings into actual, prioritized business needs.

---

## 2. Priority Hierarchy

Requirements are categorized into five strict priorities:
1. `REQUIRED`: Critical foundation without which customer acquisition fails.
2. `HIGH`: Highest-ROI growth levers for the specific business model.
3. `MEDIUM`: Supporting growth optimizations.
4. `LOW`: Baseline hygiene tasks.
5. `NOT_CURRENTLY_REQUIRED`: Intentionally excluded channels that provide low ROI for the business type.

---

## 3. The General Store Case Study

> [!TIP]
> **Missing Social ≠ Automatic Requirement**
> For a local convenience grocer / general store:
> - **Google Business Profile & Map Presence**: `HIGH` / `REQUIRED`
> - **Review Generation & Reputation**: `HIGH`
> - **Local SEO & Schema**: `MEDIUM`
> - **Instant WhatsApp Order/Inquiry**: `MEDIUM`
> - **Instagram / Facebook Content**: `NOT_CURRENTLY_REQUIRED`
> - **Paid Ads**: `NOT_CURRENTLY_REQUIRED`
>
> Services are never forced onto a customer simply to inflate subscription package size.
